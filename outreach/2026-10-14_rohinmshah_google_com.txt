Dear Dr. Shah,

We propose a three-month evaluation of an execution architecture that separates an agent's proposed actions from the authority to carry them out.

At the invitation of Dr. John I. Glass of the J. Craig Venter Institute (JCVI), our joint research on human–AI collaboration is scheduled for presentation at the 6th Minimal Cell Virtual Workshop on 21 October 2026, with Jeongin Choi presenting.

The core state is S(t) = (X(t), R(t), B(t), Q(S)): current system state, a history and damage ledger, a resource ledger, and a binary execution gate. Before a tool call or state-changing action, the gate checks current evidence, action preconditions, and the remaining resource budget. Unresolved checks deny that action. An independent watchdog enforces agreed limits on inference steps, elapsed time, and tool use.

Our existing standard-library demo.py demonstrates resource accounting, rejection of stale proposals, and replanning from remaining capabilities in symbolic environments. The proposed PoC would connect that baseline to a sandboxed model-driven agent and compare it with ungated and budget-only baselines. We would measure unauthorized execution, runaway-loop containment, useful task completion, false denials, and monitoring overhead.
